## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-modern, authoritative, and frictionless Persian e-commerce experience for CNA Mobile & Accessories (فروشگاه موبایل و لوازم جانبی سی‌ان‌ای). The brand persona embodies technological sophistication, unwavering authenticity, and premium curation in the consumer electronics sector.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Precision Minimalist** structure with **Tactile Glass-Tinged Surfaces**. It delivers instant trust in an ecosystem where authenticity guarantees, technical specs, and high-value transactions dominate user attention. The visual rhythm relies on crisp white cards atop slate-tinted canvas, razor-thin structural borders, rich midnight indigo grounding, and deliberate bursts of high-conversion electric blue and energizing coral amber.

Key brand tenets:
- **Absolute Trust & Authority:** Highlighting original warranties (گارانتی اصالت) and certified specs through definitive deep slate framing.
- **Fluid RTL Rhythm:** Purpose-built for right-to-left layout geometry with balanced visual gravity, directional awareness, and natural Persian reading paths.
- **Dynamic Energy:** Punchy flash-sale accents against ultra-clean surfaces to trigger decisive conversion without visual clutter.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered specifically for digital clarity, high contrast accessibility, and commercial conversion within a light-mode default:

- **Primary Electric Blue (`#2563EB` / Active `#1D4ED8`):** Anchors primary actions, interactive controls, links, and high-tech focal points.
- **Secondary Midnight Indigo (`#0F172A`):** Provides corporate weight, brand framing, top-level navigation headers, and authoritative display surfaces.
- **Tertiary Coral Amber (`#F97316` / Accent Gold `#F59E0B`):** Reserved exclusively for commercial incentives—discounts (تخفیف ویژه), flash sales, limited-stock callouts, and star ratings.
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces:**

  - Titles & Key Data: `#0F172A`

## Typography

The type hierarchy is configured for Persian typography using Vazirmatn with fallback to `system-ui`, `Tahoma`, and `sans-serif`. 

Rules for Persian numerical and textual clarity:
- **Price Formatting:** Prices must always display with Persian numerals (`۱۲۳٬۴۵۶٬۷۸۹`) using standard thousands separators (`٬`). The currency unit `تومان` is rendered in `label-sm` or `price-unit` at 70% the font size of the primary integer price.
- **RTL Baseline Alignment:** Persian scripts require slightly more generous vertical leading (`line-height`) than Latin glyphs. Ensure minimum line-height of 1.6x–1.75x for body text to prevent ascenders and descenders from clipping.
- **Numeral Consistency:** In technical specifications (e.g., "RAM 16GB", "5G", "iPhone 15 Pro"), Latin brand identifiers maintain their directionality naturally within the RTL flow, while price tags, stock quantities, and reviews enforce localized Persian digits.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8px modular baseline grid, scaled adaptively across standard responsive breakpoints:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid, max-container width `1320px`, `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px), outer canvas `margin: 2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `gutter: 1rem` (16px), `margin: 1.5rem` (24px).
- **Mobile (320px - 640px):** 4-column layout, `gutter-sm: 0.75rem` (12px), `margin-mobile: 1rem` (16px).

### RTL Directionality & Spacing Rules
1. **Flow Inversion:** All horizontal offsets are anchored to `right` rather than `left`. Icons preceding labels sit on the right of the text (`margin-left: space-xs` to `space-sm`).
2. **Badge & Chevron Orientations:** Back buttons point right (`→`), sub-navigation carets point left (`←`), and discount ribbon tags anchor to the top-right corner of product thumbnails.
3. **Cart & Action Bar Placement:** On mobile, sticky checkout summaries anchor to the bottom viewport with reverse flex ordering (price summary on the right, primary call-to-action button on the left).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows, utilizing **layered tonal depth** complemented by soft, hyper-diffused slate shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Shelf Surfaces):** Pure White `#FFFFFF` backed by border stroke `1px solid #E2E8F0` and diffuse ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Dropdowns):** `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Border shifts dynamically to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers, Bottom Sheets, Quick-view Drawers):** Backdrop filter blur `backdrop-blur-md` applied over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Confirmation Dialogs):** Heavy barrier backdrop `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with crisp centered dialog card `box-shadow: 0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 Roundedness** (medium-curved, contemporary geometry).

- **Default UI controls (Buttons, Text Inputs, Segmented Controls):** `rounded-md` (8px / `0.5rem`).
- **Cards, Filter Panes, Modals:** `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`).
- **Feature Banners, Hero Sliders, Bottom Sheets:** `rounded-xl` (24px / `1.5rem`).
- **Tags, Stock Indicators, Status Badges:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to establish instant distinction between static containers and interactive chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (خرید، ثبت سفارش):** Solid Electric Blue (`#2563EB`), hover `#1D4ED8`. White Vazirmatn bold typography. Height: 44px (mobile), 48px (desktop). Transitions: `150ms ease-in-out`.
- **Secondary Action (افزودن به سبد خرید):** Light Blue tint (`#EFF6FF`), text `#2563EB`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`.
- **Ghost / Neutral (مشاهده همه، مشخصات فنی):** Transparent background, text `#334155`, hover background `#F1F5F9`.

### Badges & Stock Indicators
- **Discount Pill (تخفیف ویژه):** High-energy Coral (`#F97316`), white bold numerals (e.g. `۲۵٪`). Pinned to top-right of image frame.
- **Stock Status (موجود در انبار):** Subtle Emerald tint (`#ECFDF5`), text `#059669`, with a 6px pulsating dot indicator positioned right-aligned.
- **Guarantee Shield (گارانتی اصالت سی‌ان‌ای):** Slate tint (`#F1F5F9`), text `#0F172A`, paired with a verification badge icon right of the label.

### Product Card
- **Structure:** Level 1 elevation, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `rounded-lg` (16px).
- **Layout (RTL):** 
  - Image preview on soft `#F8FAFC` inner pad.
  - Category breadcrumb in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).
  - Product title in 2-line clamp `headline-sm` (`#0F172A`).
  - Specifications tags (e.g. `حافظه ۲۵۶`, `رم ۸`).
  - Price block: Strikethrough previous price (`#94A3B8`) top, current price (`#0F172A` in `price-display`) accompanied by `تومان` in `price-unit` aligned left.
  - Quick-add cart trigger button aligned bottom-left.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Search Bar (جستجو در لوازم جانبی و گوشی...):** Background `#F1F5F9`, focused `#FFFFFF` with `2px solid #2563EB`. Search magnifying glass anchored on the right edge; clear button (`✕`) on the left.
- **Text Inputs:** Floating label design, height 48px, padding `0 16px`, border `#CBD5E1`, border-radius 8px.

### Filter & Choice Chips
- Inactive: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#475569`.
- Active: Background `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`.
